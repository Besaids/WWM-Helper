Core resources

  • Energy – Regenerates 1 every 9 real-time minutes up to your personal cap (e.g. 500). Spent on Campaign Challenge, Outpost Challenge and Sword Trial (20 Energy for Campaign/Outpost/Joint Battle, 60 Energy for boss trials like Formless Mountain Pass).
  • Stamina – Crafting resource with a 2,500 cap. You gain 450 Stamina back at each daily reset for crafting gear, food, medicine, manuals and construction items.

Daily-limited activities

  • Jianghu Errands – Sect daily missions reset here. Grant Merit plus Reputation (Reputation has its own weekly gain cap).
  • Blessed Land for Cultivation, Five Animal Frolics, Scholar's Hearth – Online activities usually tied into Jianghu Errands; their participation rewards reset daily.
  • Bounties – NPC bounty list refreshes. Once you clear all NPC bounties you can only continue with player-posted bounties (for Fourfold Coin and leaderboard progress) until next reset.
  • Ye Buxiu Betting Match (Chess) – Located in Qinghe – Dreamscape: Blissful Retreat. You can win up to 3 times per day. On Expert difficulty that is up to 450 Commerce Coin for 3 wins; each loss costs 150 Commerce Coin.

Merchant daily stock

Nice extras around reset

Weekly group content

What the Mirage Boat is

  • Premium, ultra-rare boat mount unlocked by collecting 170 Mirage Torn Pages from the Celestial Draw and exchanging them in the Draw Shop.
  • Lets the owner start a server-wide social event called the Painted Cruise / Mirage Boat Event, effectively a moving party on the rivers around Kaifeng.
  • Mostly cosmetic and social; it is a flex mount and an event trigger rather than a direct power boost.

When the event runs

  • There is a fixed weekly cruise every Sunday evening and Monday morning (UTC; check this timer's countdown and the in-game event panel for the exact times on your server).
  • Mirage Boat owners can also spend currency to trigger extra cruises outside the fixed windows, so you may occasionally see additional departures.

How to join

  • When a cruise is active, an event icon appears on your UI; follow it to the dock and board the Mirage Boat with other players.
  • The boat follows a set route; you can roam the deck, emote, and join small mini-activities while it sails.
  • You do not need to own the Mirage Boat to participate; any player can join as a guest when a cruise is running.

Rewards and perks

  • You earn Fortune Points simply by staying on the boat and participating in its mini-games; at 1,000 Fortune Points you can claim your reward via the Mirage Boat icon at the top-right of the screen.
  • Rewards are mailed to you if you leave early; the quality scales with how much the owner spends to fund the cruise.
  • During the event you can temporarily wear any outfits owned by the boat's owner, letting you test premium cosmetics for free while you're on board.
