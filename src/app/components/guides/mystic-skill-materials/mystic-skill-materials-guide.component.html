Mystic Skill Materials & Gathering

Learn how to see which plant each mystic skill needs and how to farm those plants efficiently. All mystic skill upgrades use Ebon Iron plus a specific plant material, and this guide covers solo and online gathering strategies.

What Mystic Skills Use

Mystic skills consume Ebon Iron (Lv.1–4) for most upgrade ranks. Each mystic skill is also tied to one specific plant used for breakthrough levels. For example, Jade Tower Peony gathering can yield a rare drop called Jade Tower Pearl, which is then used in the upgrading process alongside Ebon Iron.

Plants can be gathered in two modes: Solo World has long respawn times but no competition, while Online World offers shorter respawn timers but nodes are shared with other players.

Checking Required Materials

In the Mystic Skills menu, when you select a skill and view an upgrade, the panel shows the required Ebon Iron level and quantity, plus the specific plant name and icon. Each upgrade panel also includes a "Gather <plant name>" button (for example, "Gather Jade Tower Peony").

Pressing the Gather button auto-tracks the nearest node and keeps updating the HUD marker as you collect and exhaust nodes, making it easy to hop from node to node cluster without manually searching the map.

Solo Gathering on Foot (No Horse)

When gathering on foot, you must manually find and pick each plant. There is no auto-vacuum. The basic loop is: use the tracked HUD marker from the Gather button to run to the nearest node cluster and harvest all the plants from it. After harvesting, the marker automatically jumps to the next nearest node cluster of that plant.

Using Wind Sense

Wind Sense (key V) is a visual aid only; it highlights nearby gatherable objects but does not collect them. Use Wind Sense when you can't immediately identify the plants visually. It makes scanning the scene easier by highlighting nodes, but you still need to walk up to each node and interact with it to harvest.

Solo World Respawn Timers

In Solo World, once you begin farming a specific plant, treat it as a once-per-day loop in that world. Find a route that feels efficient for you instead of camping a single node, as the respawn times are quite long.

Speeding Up with Spirited Courser (Mount)

The mount Spirited Courser has a gather skill called Petals Collection that collects all nearby gatherable nodes within a radius. This skill has a short cooldown (10 seconds in the screenshot). This behavior is specific to Spirited Courser and its Petals Collection skill—it does not work when gathering on foot.

Spirited Courser can be purchased from the mount shop and costs Echo Beads. So overall it has a real money cost to adquire this horse, it's just a ROI item.

On foot: You run to each plant and collect it individually.
On Spirited Courser: Ride into the middle of the highlighted area, hit the Petals Collection skill once (default key Z), and let the mount vacuum all plants around you before moving on to the next tracked node. (sometimes you may need to use it more than once depending on how sparsely located are the flowers in the cluster)

Gathering Buff Food (Commerce Coins)

Two food items provide critical gathering buffs and cost Commerce Coins:

  • Snail Meat – 15% critical gathering, 20-minute duration.
  • Chicken Noodles – 20% critical gathering, 10-minute duration.

"Critical gathering" means higher chances to get extra or rarer materials from each harvested node. Snail Meat offers a slightly weaker buff but longer duration, making it good for long, relaxed plant loops. Chicken Noodles provides a stronger buff with a short duration, ideal for quick focused bursts when you just need a few more plants.

Plan Your Mystic Skill Upgrades

Once you know how to gather materials efficiently, a future Mystic Skill Upgrade Planner tool will help calculate how many plants and how much Ebon Iron you need for each tier and breakthrough, making it easier to plan your farming routes and prioritize upgrades.
