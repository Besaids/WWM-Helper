How Boss Talents Work

Boss Talents are seasonal challenges found under Talents → Boss in the game menu. They provide permanent stat bonuses that apply only in Sword Trial and Hero's Realm content.

{{ season.disclaimer }}

Track Types

  • Offensive – Increases your damage vs the boss, usually during its Exhausted state.
  • Defensive – Reduces damage from key boss mechanics or improves survivability.
  • Strategic – Generic damage/healing bonuses or challenge-mode clear counts.

Unlocking Hero Titles

Each boss has an associated hero title that is unlocked by completing all tiers of both the Offensive and Defensive tracks. The Strategic track is optional for title unlocks but still provides useful bonuses and other rewards.
